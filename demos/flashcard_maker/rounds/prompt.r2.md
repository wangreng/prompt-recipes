Build a minimalist single-page Flashcard Generator web app powered by the Gemini API. Users enter a topic (like "Ancient Rome") or "Term: Definition" pairs (one per line) to generate interactive clickable flashcards. Keep the interface uncluttered with no navbar, sidebar, footer, sample cards, or extra controls.

Use a Google Material-inspired style loading Google Sans (400, 500) as the primary font—falling back to Roboto and sans-serif—with natural, uncondensed letter spacing and automatic system light/dark mode. Use an ultra-light, near-white off-white page background (#f8f9fa). Near the top, center a generously wide white card (~860–880px on desktop) with ~40px side/bottom padding, extra top room (~60px above the heading), subtly rounded corners (~12px), and a very light layered drop shadow. In dark mode, switch to Google dark charcoal surfaces, light gray text, soft sky-blue accents (dark button text), and dark gray textarea and card fills.

Stack these elements in a centered column with comfortable vertical spacing:
- A Google Blue "Flashcard Generator" heading (~32px, medium weight, not bold).
- A muted-gray subtitle (~16px, regular weight): "Enter a topic or a list of terms and definitions to generate flashcards using the Gemini API."
- A nearly full-width, vertically resizable textarea of moderate height (~120–140px, about 4–5 lines) with rounded corners, near-white fill, thin border, blue focus ring, and 16px monospace text so the placeholder "Enter topic (e.g., Ancient Rome) or 'Term: Definition' pairs (one per line)" spans most of the box width.
- A centered Google Blue "Generate Flashcards" button with generous padding (~14px by 24px), moderately rounded corners (~8px), standard 16px medium-weight white text (not bold or small), hover elevation, and a muted disabled state while loading.
- Spacious empty white room inside the card below the button (~120px when empty), by keeping a reserved-height Google Red status/error line and the centered wrapping flashcard grid (with a wide top margin) always in the layout so nothing jumps.

On click, validate non-empty input; if blank, clear cards and ask for a topic or terms. Otherwise clear old cards, disable the button, show "Generating flashcards...", and ask Gemini for concise "Term: Definition" pairs (one per line). Render each valid pair as a compact landscape card (~220x150px) with a pale ice-blue surface (dark gray in dark mode), thin border, and soft shadow deepening with a blue border on hover. Show the centered term on the front in larger medium-weight text, flipping smoothly in 3D on click to reveal the definition in smaller muted text. Show clear messages for empty, malformed, or failed responses, and always re-enable the button.
