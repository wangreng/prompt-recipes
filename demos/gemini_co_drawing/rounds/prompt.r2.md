Build a minimal collaborative sketching web app called "Gemini Co-Drawing" powered by Gemini 2.0 native image generation, where a user draws on a canvas and prompts Gemini to edit or build on their sketch in a continuous loop.

Use a crisp light-mode design on a pure white background with a single centered column around 1024px max width (with 24px horizontal padding so clear white side margins remain on desktop), moderate 40px top padding, and ample bottom breathing room below the input.

At the top, place a header row aligned to the canvas edges with a 24px gap beneath it, stacking cleanly on mobile. On the left, put a moderately sized bold dark-charcoal sans-serif title (~30px on desktop, not oversized) reading "Gemini Co-Drawing", followed by two regular body-sized (~16px) muted medium-gray subtitle lines with a slight gap between them and underlined links: "Built with Gemini 2.0 native image generation" (linking to the Gemini API image generation docs) and "by @trudypainter and @alexanderchen" (linking to their X profiles). On the right, bottom-aligned so it spans roughly the height of both subtitle lines, place a medium cool-gray pill toolbar (distinct silver-gray, not pale off-white, with 8px padding and subtle shadow) holding two 40px circular buttons 8px apart: a pen-color swatch (black by default) with a 2px white ring opening the native color picker on click or keyboard activation, and a white circle with a dark-gray trash icon. Add a slight hover zoom on both.

Below the header, place a full-width 16:9 white drawing canvas (~60% of desktop viewport height, shorter on mobile, 320px minimum) with sharp corners, a 2px solid black border, and a crosshair cursor. Support smooth mouse and touch sketching—blocking touch scroll while drawing—with medium rounded 5px strokes in the active pen color.

Leave a 24px gap below the canvas, then place a full-width, generously padded single-line text input (~60px tall with 16px padding), sharp corners, a matching 2px black border, subtle shadow, body-sized monospace text, and placeholder "Add your change...". Inset 16px from its right edge, vertically center a 40px solid-black sharp-cornered submit button with a 24px white horizontal send icon.

Submitting a required prompt disables the button with a spinning loader, captures the canvas over a white background as a PNG, and sends it with the prompt—telling Gemini to keep the same minimal line doodle style—to Gemini's native image generation model. Draw the returned image onto the canvas for continuous sketching on top. Clicking clear resets the canvas and generated image to blank white. On errors, show a centered white rounded modal over a dimmed backdrop titled "Failed to generate" with a close icon.
