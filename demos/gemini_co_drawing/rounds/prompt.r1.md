Build a minimal collaborative sketching web app called "Gemini Co-Drawing" powered by Gemini 2.0 native image generation, where a user draws on a canvas and prompts Gemini to edit or build on their sketch in a continuous loop.

Use a crisp light-mode aesthetic on a pure white background with a single centered column, generous top padding, and clean vertical spacing. At the top, place a header row aligned to the edges of the canvas that stacks neatly on mobile. On the left, show a bold dark sans-serif heading reading "Gemini Co-Drawing", followed by two smaller muted-gray subtitle lines with underlined links: "Built with Gemini 2.0 native image generation" (linking to the Gemini API image generation docs) and "by @trudypainter and @alexanderchen" (linking to their X profiles). On the right, bottom-aligned with the text, place a compact light-gray pill toolbar with a subtle shadow holding two circular buttons side by side: a color-swatch button filled with the active pen color (black by default) and outlined with a thin white ring that opens the native color picker on click or keyboard activation, and a white circular button with a dark-gray trash icon to clear the canvas. Give both buttons a slight hover zoom.

Below the header, place a full-width widescreen white drawing canvas (about 60% of viewport height on desktop, shorter on mobile) with sharp corners, a crisp 2px solid black border, and a crosshair cursor. Support smooth freehand drawing for mouse and touch—blocking touch scrolling while sketching—using medium rounded strokes in the chosen pen color.

Below the canvas, place a matching full-width single-line text input with sharp corners, a matching 2px black border, monospace typography, and placeholder "Add your change...". Embed a small square solid-black submit button inside its right edge with a white horizontal send icon.

When the user submits a required prompt, disable the button with a spinning loader, capture the canvas on a white background as a PNG, and send it with the prompt—instructing Gemini to keep the same minimal line doodle style—to Gemini's native image generation model. Draw the returned image onto the canvas so the user can keep sketching on top and iterating. Clicking clear resets the canvas and generated image to blank white. On request errors, show a centered white rounded modal over a dimmed backdrop titled "Failed to generate" with a close icon.
