Build a nostalgic Windows 95 desktop simulator called "Gemini 95" filled with classic 90s apps reimagined with Gemini AI.

Start on a full-screen classic Windows 95 teal desktop with no scrollbars or open windows. Near the top-left, place a horizontal row of seven retro pixel-art icons with generous spacing and white shadowed labels: My Gemtop, Chrome, GemNotes, GemPaint, Doom II, Gemini Chat, and GemSweeper. Fix a light-gray taskbar along the bottom with a raised 3D "Gemini" Start button on the left toggling a Start menu of all seven apps (navy-blue hover highlight), plus beveled buttons for open apps that look pressed when active.

Give every window classic light-gray chrome, 3D beveled borders, navy-blue title bars with bold white titles, sunken content areas, and beveled minimize and close buttons. Use Inter for the OS UI, Courier New for Notepad and game counters, Times New Roman for browser controls, and Comic Sans for playful AI quips. Single-clicking any icon or Start menu item opens or restores its window; windows are draggable by their title bars, resizable, focus on click, and toggle from the taskbar.

Include these apps:
- My Gemtop: A white file explorer with a C: drive revealing a secret file (dontshowthistoanyone.jpg) that opens an easter-egg photo in an Image Viewer window.
- Chrome: A retro browser with a URL bar and "Go!" button starting on an archived 1999 Google page. Visiting any URL plays a dial-up sound and connecting screen while Gemini generates a chaotic 1996-style webpage with a custom retro logo image, neon colors, marquees, blinking text, and a visitor counter.
- GemNotes: A monospace notepad with a "Generate Story" button that streams a short twist-ending story from Gemini.
- GemPaint: A drawing canvas with color swatches, an eraser, S/M/L brushes, and Clear. While open, a Clippy-style robot appears in the bottom-right with a pale-yellow speech bubble, periodically using Gemini vision to give witty, sarcastic art critiques.
- Doom II: Playable Doom embedded in an iframe.
- Gemini Chat: A retro chat window with streaming replies (blue user text, green AI text).
- GemSweeper: A 9x9 Minesweeper game with red-on-black digital flag and timer counters, a reactive smiley reset button, and a "Hint" button where Gemini offers witty board commentary.
