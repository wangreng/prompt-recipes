# Design Recipe

## Mood & Core Idea
**One-sentence design idea:** Pair a single poetic, painterly sunset opening with a razor-sharp, nearly monochrome dark-mode gallery of real software interfaces.

The mood is calm, nocturnal, and quietly confident — stepping from an open dusk horizon into a craftsman's darkroom. It feels built for thoughtful technical builders, makers, and power users who value visual craft over loud marketing hype.

## Typography Logic
Pair two well-known free font families:
- **Opening Display Serif (`Source Serif 4`):** Reserved exclusively for the hero headline. Medium weight, sentence case, tight leading, balanced wrapping at three to four times compact UI text size, with a soft dark shadow over the sky art. Using a serif only once creates an editorial entrance.
- **Interface & Section Sans (`Inter`, with `Geist` in the footer):** Drives all section headings, card titles, body copy, navigation, buttons, and UI demos. Section headings use medium weight, sentence case, and tight negative letter-spacing at roughly two-and-a-half times body size.
- **Casing & Setting:** Sentence case throughout; uppercase appears only on tiny utility codes like a locale tag. Body copy uses generous line-height and a restrained measure; counters, timestamps, and prices use tabular numerals.

## Color Logic
- **Atmospheric Hero:** Lush color lives solely in the full-bleed dusk illustration — deep cool-blue zenith warming into coral, peach, and soft violet along the horizon — before dissolving through a dark bottom fade into the page canvas.
- **Monochrome Dark Gallery:** Everything below runs on a strict neutral dark ladder: near-black obsidian canvas, slightly lifted matte charcoal cards and tiles, hairline translucent white borders, bright off-white primary ink, and cool medium-gray secondary ink.
- **Actions & Semantic Pops:** Primary actions use inverted monochrome (solid white pills with dark ink) rather than brand fills. Secondary hero actions use frosted translucent glass over the sky, switching to dark-tinted outlined pills on the dark canvas. Chromatic color below the hero stays quarantined inside miniature UI proofs (window dots, active toggles, message bubbles, third-party marks).

## Spacing & Density Rhythm
The hero spans nearly the opening viewport with generous sky above the centered headline. The focal product preview overlaps the hero's bottom fade via an upward pull, bridging story and utility seamlessly. Below the fold, sections pace evenly inside a centered medium-wide container with tidy card padding, expanding into very generous vertical whitespace in the closing band.

## Grid & Layout Moves
Every section opens on a centered axis with a narrow-measure heading and short subtitle before varying grid rhythm: from one wide overlapping frame, to paired copy-and-vignette blocks, to equal-height three- and four-column portrait grids, to aligned comparison columns. Equalized card-header heights align nested visual frames horizontally, while flexible checklists pin bottom buttons cleanly. The footer pairs a brand summary left with a multi-column directory right above a slim baseline bar.

## Component Vocabulary
- **Navigation:** Slim transparent overlay over the hero sky. Left groups a white mark, wordmark, and quiet text links; right holds compact utilities (community counter, icon link, locale switcher) with no navbar CTA.
- **Buttons:** Hero and closing bands pair full-pill buttons: a solid white primary pill with trailing arrow and a secondary split-pill with attached chevron dropdown. Inside cards, actions become full-width, softly rounded rectangles.
- **Cards & Proof Frames:** Flat matte charcoal outer cards with generous rounding enclose tighter-rounded dark UI frames outlined by hairlines. Comparison cards pair title, optional muted badge, short summary, large tabular figure, two-tone checkmark list (key specs bright, supporting items muted), and full-width button; the highlighted tier earns a crisp brighter border without scale distortion.
- **Icons:** Fine monochrome line icons for controls; full-color marks only inside ecosystem tiles.

## Imagery & Illustration Style
Contrast one painterly cinematic dusk landscape in the hero (wide horizon, glowing sky, tranquil depth) against authentic dark-mode UI mockups below. Every claim is proven through realistic windows, threads, or lists rather than decorative spot art.

## Motion & Interaction Feel
Transitions stay brisk and quiet: hover brightening, subtle chevron flips, and clear focus rings. Embedded vignettes animate step-by-step with a gliding simulated cursor and unobtrusive pause/replay controls. An optional ambient interactive epilogue below the footer rewards curious scrollers.

## What This Site Deliberately Does NOT Do
- No neon glows, purple-cyan gradients, aurora blobs, or glowing card borders.
- No sign-up or primary action button in the top header.
- No uppercase eyebrows, badge clutter above headings, or heavy drop-shadow stacks.
- No stock photos, abstract 3D shapes, testimonial carousels, or logo walls.
- No scaled-up featured pricing cards or loud promotional ribbons.

## Page Skeleton
1. **Transparent Overlay Header:** Brand lockup, primary links, compact community and locale utilities.
2. **Illustrated Hero + Overlapping Showcase:** Full-bleed dusk banner, centered serif headline, subtitle, paired pill CTAs, overlapping interactive window.
3. **Deep-Dive Capability Showcase:** Centered intro and four copy-plus-animated-window vignette blocks.
4. **Three-Pillar Proof Bento:** Centered intro and three equal cards with tall portrait UI snapshots.
5. **Multi-Context Strip:** Centered intro, four compact portrait preview cards with captions, and a centered row of integration tiles.
6. **Three-Tier Comparison Grid:** Centered heading and three aligned plan cards with understated featured-border emphasis.
7. **Minimal Closing Call-to-Action:** Spacious centered invitation repeating the paired pills.
8. **Multi-Column Footer (+ Optional Epilogue):** Brand summary, grouped links, copyright/social bar, and optional ambient epilogue.
