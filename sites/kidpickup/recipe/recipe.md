# Design Recipe: Sunlit Storybook Utility

## Mood, Core Idea & Audience
Warm, sunlit, plainspoken, and human—like a favorite picture book paired with an indie maker’s zine.

**Design idea:** Combine warm pastel color-blocking and sketchy editorial character art with friendly geometric typography and gently tilted product previews so everyday software feels calm, honest, and approachable.

Built for busy, non-technical people across generations who distrust hype, ads, and sterile corporate polish, and value warmth and clarity.

## Typography
Use **Plus Jakarta Sans** throughout—bold display weights for headings, regular for prose. Keep strong scale contrast: a monumental wordmark fills the first screen; section headlines sit three to four times body size in heavy bold with tight tracking and compact leading; card titles sit about one-and-a-half times body size; body copy stays light with airy leading.

Set headings, nav, cards, and buttons in sentence case. Reserve bold tracked uppercase solely for small pill eyebrows and tiny comparison tags. Punctuate display headlines with periods, swipe key opening phrases with soft pastel highlights, and use inline bold sparingly.

## Color Logic
Alternate warm butter-cream (default canvas) and crisp white bands. Avoid pure black; set primary ink and icons in deep muted plum-charcoal, and secondary copy in dusty plum-slate.

Pace sections with six chalky pastels—sunshine yellow, sky blue, soft mint, lilac, warm peach, and blush pink—across bands, card fills, and icon badges under dark plum ink.

Strict accent roles:
- **Cobalt-periwinkle:** hero wordmark only.
- **Warm coral-vermilion:** primary CTA pills and urgent buttons, uniquely set with dark plum ink rather than white.
- **Golden yellow:** eyebrow pills, featured cards, text highlights, and rating stars.
- **Semantic pairs:** cream/blush with terracotta tags ("before") paired with mint and forest-green tags ("after").

## Spacing & Rhythm
Give the hero expansive negative space across the opening viewport. Pad each full-bleed band generously so scrolling feels like turning pages. Alternate airy narrative splits with denser scannable grids, returning to calm single-column reading for trust, pricing, and Q&A. Keep generous card padding.

## Grid & Layout Moves
Center content in a comfortable wide measure using four moves:
1. **Monumental hero** flowing on butter-cream into a two-column problem/solution spread and centered CTA row.
2. **Asymmetric splits** pairing left copy (eyebrow, headline, lead, two-by-two sub-points, optional CTA/link) with a right visual (tilted phone, schematic wireframe, or floating alert card).
3. **Pastel card matrices**—split-tone comparisons, tall numbered walkthrough columns, two-by-two pillar tiles, three-by-three illustrated persona cards, dense three-column white micro-cards, and a lead-weighted article grid.
4. **Asymmetric trust spreads** pairing a grounded heading or invitation beside stacked white cards.

## Component Vocabulary
- **Nav:** Translucent butter-cream bar with centered bold links and no CTA; brand mark hides while the hero wordmark shows, collapsing on mobile to a two-line toggle.
- **Buttons:** Fully pill-shaped. Primary coral pills use dark plum ink; secondary white pills have thin plum borders, soft shadows, and optional icons or arrows. Store badges sit alongside, with upcoming platforms tagged by a tiny golden pill below.
- **Cards & Icons:** Generously rounded pastel or white cards with hairline plum borders and diffuse shadows; thin rounded plum line icons, bare or inside pastel rounded-square badges.
- **Previews:** Dark-bezel phone mockups (often tilted with floating pill stickers) plus minimal white-and-pastel schematic wireframes.
- **Consent Notice:** Compact rounded white bottom-left card with twin secondary pills.

## Imagery & Illustration
Human scenes use hand-drawn ink-and-watercolor editorial art—delicate dark outlines, translucent pastel washes, expressive characters, and dotted trails—sitting transparently on colored grounds with no photo boxes. Hero figures perch on and lean against the wordmark while another peeks from the corner. Realistic visuals are limited to UI inside mockups and a framed video.

## Motion & Interaction
Keep motion storybook-light. Hero figures run gentle staggered sway, bob, and lean loops. Selected mockups, stickers, and the featured comparison card sit at slight tilts. Rely on light hover shifts, native accordions, and inline video—never scroll choreography.

## Deliberate Omissions
- No stock photography, dark-mode hero, neon gradients, glassmorphism, or glossy 3D renders.
- No pure black text or sterile gray minimalism.
- No dark patterns: no pop-ups, countdowns, sticky promo bars, or gated pricing.
- No mega-menus, top-bar CTA clutter, or scroll-hijacking.

## Page Skeleton
1. **Centered Minimalist Nav** + corner consent card.
2. **Illustrated Wordmark Hero** (perched figures, tagline, video pill, store badges).
3. **Problem/Solution Intro** (highlighted two-column spread, CTA, plain terms, scene + tilted phone with stickers).
4. **Walkthrough Video** (sunshine yellow, framed player).
5. **Feature Deep-Dive Split** (pastel, exclusion pills, sub-points, tilted phone).
6. **Before/After Grid** (white, three-by-two split-tone cards, CTA).
7. **Three-Step Walkthrough** (butter-cream, tall pastel columns: mockups, vignettes, step badges).
8. **Category Comparison** (butter-cream, illustration over three pastel cards; gold product card tilted).
9. **Core Pillars** (white, two-by-two pastel icon tiles).
10. **Platform & Workflow Splits** (pastel splits with wireframes and tilted alert card).
11. **Audience Showcase** (white, three-by-three illustrated pastel cards).
12. **Capability Directory** (butter-cream, dense three-column white micro-cards + archive pill).
13. **Privacy Split** (butter-cream, heading + stacked white cards).
14. **Pricing** (white, two pastel tiers, shared-features box, pledges, CTA).
15. **FAQ Stack** (butter-cream, white accordions, first open).
16. **Maker Note** (wide peach card, illustration, signed letter).
17. **Social Proof** (mint, star-rated reviews + story invitation).
18. **Articles Grid** (white, lead-weighted pastel-header cards + archive pill).
19. **Closing CTA & Footer** (white footer, top sunshine-yellow CTA card, four-column sitemap, attribution, languages, socials, credit).
