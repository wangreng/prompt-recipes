# Product Brief: Kid Pickup

## Product Overview
**Product Name:** Kid Pickup  
**Category:** Consumer mobile and web application for family logistics and shared child-pickup scheduling.  
**Maker / Publisher:** Created by independent developer Joel Wood (Think X Design Media), based on Vancouver Island, British Columbia, Canada, serving families in Canada and the United States.  
**Platforms:** Native iPhone app (available on the Apple App Store, including compatibility with Apple’s foldable iPhone Duo), companion web browser application (`app.kidpickup.com`), and an Android version listed as upcoming.  
**Core Value Proposition:** Eliminates missed or ambiguous child pickups and drop-offs by replacing scattered family group chats with a single shared calendar where every ride is explicitly claimed by one named adult, paired with automatic escalation alerts whenever a pickup remains unassigned.

## Target Audience
Parents, guardians, and the broader caregiving circle involved in transporting children to school, daycare, and extracurriculars—including dual-parent homes, solo parents, separated or blended families, grandparents, babysitters, nannies, and carpooling households.

## Brand & Voice Direction
- Direct, warm, plainspoken, and reassuring; written in everyday language without corporate jargon or exaggerated promises.
- Emphasizes honesty, transparency around billing and cancellation, and strict respect for family privacy (explicitly rejecting GPS location tracking, advertising, and data brokering).
- Positions itself clearly as a family coordination tool—distinct from passive shared calendars (which cannot assign ownership) and school-run dismissal systems (which only manage gate authorization).

## Pricing & Trial Model
- **Free Trial:** Two-week free trial for new subscribers via Apple’s App Store (requires a payment method and auto-renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours prior).
- **Family Tier (Highlighted as most common):** Annual USD billing ($59.99/year, 17% annual discount) or monthly ($5.99/month). Covers up to 2 children, 2 parents/guardians, and 3 helpers (such as grandparents or sitters); excludes the AI assistant.
- **Premium Tier:** Annual USD billing ($109.99/year, 24% annual discount) or monthly ($11.99/month). Covers unlimited children, up to 8 combined parents/guardians/helpers, and includes the AI scheduling query assistant.
- **Billing Commitments:** Advance notice (30–60 days) before price or plan changes; self-serve cancellation via App Store settings with no retention friction; one subscription covers both iOS and browser access; invited co-parents and helpers do not pay separately.
