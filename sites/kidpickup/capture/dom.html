FAQ

Questions parents ask

What happens if nobody claims a pickup?

It stands out in amber, marked OPEN, on everyone’s calendar, and the badge changes to “NEEDS HELPER” inside 24 hours. Parents and guardians get a notification 24 hours before, a more urgent one 3 hours before, and another at 2 hours, each with an I’ll do it button. The family owner can move the 3-hour one, or turn it off.

Can Kid Pickup tell when I’m at work?

If you add your work hours, yes, and a parent can add a partner’s hours too. When a pickup nobody has taken yet falls within half an hour of a parent’s shift, the pickup shows who’s at work then, with a button to ask the rest of the family. Connect your Google Calendar and a meeting counts as well, without anyone seeing what it is. It never uses your location.

Will it warn me if I double-book a child?

Yes. If the same child already has something starting within an hour that day, the app asks before it saves. You can change it, or create it anyway if that’s what you meant.

Can I add a pickup without filling in a form?

Yes. Type it the way you’d text it, like “Swim pickup for Leo Thursday at 4:30, ask Grandma”, or say it with your keyboard’s microphone, and the form fills itself in. Nothing saves until you check it and tap Create. It’s on every plan, for parents and guardians.

What if someone can’t do a pickup they were asked to do?

They tap Can’t make it, right on the notification. The pickup stays open, the heads-ups keep going, and everyone who could take it hears that it still needs someone.

Can we message each other in the app?

Yes. The Chat tab is one conversation for your whole circle, grandparents and sitters included, on every plan. Everyone else gets a notification when someone writes, and with the chat open, new messages show up within seconds. On Premium, each pickup also has its own thread.

Does the other parent have to use it?

No. Kid Pickup works with one parent and the people you invite. Your co-parent can join if you both want that.

Does Kid Pickup work for single parents?

Yes. Set up your family on your own and invite the people who help: your parents, friends, neighbors or a sitter. They take the pickups they can do, and if one is still open the app sends you a nudge in time to sort it out.

Is there a co-parenting app where only one parent pays?

Yes. With Kid Pickup, a co-parent you invite doesn’t pay. You can also use it on your own, with the grandparents, partner and sitter who help your household. It’s for covering pickups, not court records.

Can I share my kids’ schedule with a nanny or sitter?

Yes. Invite them as a nanny or babysitter and they see the pickups that are theirs or still need someone, plus the kids on those, not your whole week. You can let them see the whole calendar from their profile. They’re in the family chat too.

Is Kid Pickup a Cozi alternative?

For pickups and the kids’ schedule, yes. Cozi is a family organizer with shopping lists and meal plans. Kid Pickup gets one person to claim each pickup, keeps every activity on one calendar, and has a family chat. It doesn’t do lists or meals, so some families use both.

Does Kid Pickup work with Google Calendar?

Yes. A parent or guardian can connect their Google account under Settings, Google Calendar. Each calendar can count as busy time only, so a pickup that clashes says you have something on without saying what, or go on the family calendar for the kids you pick. Google Tasks lists can go into To do. Kid Pickup only reads from Google and never changes anything there.

Can I use Kid Pickup on my computer?

Yes. Sign in at app.kidpickup.com in a web browser, with the same account as on your iPhone, and your family’s calendar, feed and chat are there. On a computer you can drag a pickup to a new time or day. Plans are bought on iPhone and count in both, and notifications come to the iPhone.

Does Kid Pickup work in Canada?

Yes. Kid Pickup is made on Vancouver Island, BC, for families in Canada and the US. It runs on iPhone, and the App Store shows the price in Canadian dollars. Choose your province under Settings, Public Holidays, and its statutory holidays show on the calendar.

Does Kid Pickup track my location?

No. The app never asks for location permission. Saved places are just addresses you type in.

Is Kid Pickup a school dismissal system?

No. Kid Pickup is not a school dismissal system. Your school’s app runs the car line. Kid Pickup is for your family: who’s going, at every school, rink and studio.

What can the people I invite see?

Parents and guardians you invite see the whole family schedule and can change it. Grandparents see the whole calendar but can’t change it. Sitters and other helpers see only open pickups and the ones they’ve taken, plus the kids on those. You can switch any helper either way from their profile in Family. Only parents and guardians see your work hours, other people’s phone numbers and a child’s notes. Everyone sees your emergency contacts, and you can remove anyone at any time.

What happens when the free trial ends?

The two-week trial is for new subscribers. You start it by choosing a plan in the app, and Apple asks for a payment method. When the two weeks are up, Apple charges for that plan unless you cancelled at least 24 hours before, in your iPhone’s subscription settings. Your family’s schedule is kept either way.

Is Kid Pickup on Android?

Not as an app yet. It’s on iPhone, and anyone in your family can also sign in at app.kidpickup.com in a web browser, though a browser doesn’t get notifications. Android is on the way.

What does the AI assistant do?

On Premium, you can ask about your family’s pickups in plain words, like “Which pickups still need someone?” It answers from your calendar. It doesn’t make changes for you. Typing a new pickup as a sentence is a separate feature, on every plan.

How do I cancel?

Any time, on your iPhone: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before your trial, month or year ends. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and there’s no cancellation fee.
