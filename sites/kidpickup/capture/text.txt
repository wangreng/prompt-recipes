A NOTE FROM THE PERSON WHO BUILT IT

I’m Joel. I’m a dad of three on Vancouver Island and a soccer coach, so most of my week is somebody getting somebody somewhere. I built Kid Pickup because the group chat kept failing us. Someone would ask who was getting the kids, a few people would reply, and it still wasn’t clear who had actually said yes.

Most weeks it worked out. I wanted something for the weeks it didn’t: one place where every pickup has a name on it, and a nudge when one doesn’t.

It’s a small app made by one family’s worth of frustration. I read every message. If something’s broken or missing, tell me through the contact form.
