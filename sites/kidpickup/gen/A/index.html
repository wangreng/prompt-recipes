Frequently Asked Questions

Plain answers to practical questions.

When do unclaimed pickup escalation alerts go out? +

Whenever a pickup or drop-off still has no assigned driver, Kid Pickup automatically alerts parents at 24 hours, 3 hours, and 2 hours before the scheduled time so there is always plenty of breathing room to arrange coverage.

How do work-hour and Google Calendar conflict checks work? +

You enter your work hours once (visible only to parents/guardians) and optionally connect a read-only Google Calendar overlay. Kid Pickup automatically adds a 30-minute travel buffer around work shifts and flags any pickup that overlaps so you can ask your circle for help in one tap.

Will Kid Pickup warn me if one person is double-booked? +

Yes. If the same adult is assigned to two pickups within one hour of each other, Kid Pickup highlights a double-booking clash warning right away so nobody is expected at two fields at once.

How does natural-language text or voice event entry work? +

Just type or dictate something like “Leo soccer pickup Thursday at 5:15 pm at Oak Park” and Kid Pickup fills in the child, date, time, activity icon, and saved place automatically.

What happens if a grandparent or helper declines a pickup request? +

Declining is one polite tap. Parents are notified immediately, the pickup stays visibly marked as open, and escalation reminders remain active until someone else claims it.

Does Kid Pickup include in-app family chat? +

Yes. You get both whole-circle chat and private parent-only threads with photo sharing and read/delivery receipts, keeping practical pickup talk right beside the calendar.

How does billing work for solo parents or separated co-parents? +

One single subscription covers the whole family circle. Solo parents can invite grandparents, neighbors, and sitters for free, and separated co-parents share one subscription without paying twice.

What can sitters and occasional helpers see? +

Helpers only see the children and schedule details you choose to share with their role. Private parent work hours, parent-only chat threads, and billing stay completely hidden, and you can remove a helper anytime.

How is Kid Pickup different from Cozi? +

Cozi is a general household organizer with ads on its free tier and no pickup-claiming workflow. Kid Pickup is completely ad-free and purpose-built for transportation ownership: explicit accept/decline buttons, 24h/3h/2h gap escalations, driver reconfirmation on time changes, and arrival check-ins.

Does it work with Google Calendar? +

Yes — in both directions. You can display a read-only import of your Google Calendar and Tasks inside Kid Pickup to spot conflicts, and subscribe to your Kid Pickup schedule from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook.

Can I use Kid Pickup on my computer? +

Yes. Sign in at app.kidpickup.com from any desktop browser using your existing account for drag-and-drop week planning and sidebar navigation. Push notifications stay on your iPhone.

Does Kid Pickup support Canadian families and holidays? +

Absolutely — Kid Pickup is made on Vancouver Island, British Columbia! Both Canadian and U.S. regional public holidays, school breaks, and local App Store CAD/USD billing are supported natively.

Does Kid Pickup track my child’s or driver’s GPS location? +

Never. Kid Pickup does not collect GPS coordinates or track live maps. Instead, drivers tap simple “Arrived” and “Picked up” buttons that send calm confirmation updates to parents.

How is this different from my school’s dismissal app? +

School dismissal apps manage curb authorization for school staff at 3:00 p.m. Kid Pickup coordinates your family beforehand — deciding which parent, grandparent, or sitter is actually driving to school, daycare, soccer, and piano.

How do role-based visibility rules work? +

Parents and guardians have full visibility and configurable edit permissions. Helpers have scoped access tailored to the kids they help with and receive quieter default notifications so their phones aren’t buzzed unnecessarily.

What happens when my 14-day free trial ends? +

Your two-week free trial is managed through Apple’s App Store and requires a payment method up front. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial finishes, it automatically renews into the plan you selected.

When is the Android app coming out? +

An Android app is actively planned and listed as upcoming. In the meantime, relatives or co-parents on Android can already use app.kidpickup.com in their mobile browser or claim rides with one tap via texted web claim links — no app required.

What does the Premium AI assistant actually do? +

Available on the Premium plan, the scheduling query assistant lets you ask plain questions about your own family schedule — like “Which afternoons are still uncovered next week?” — without digging through multiple days. Your family data is never used to train external AI models.

Can helpers claim a pickup without downloading an app? +

Yes. You can text a secure one-tap web claim link to any grandparent, friend, or occasional carpool driver. They open the link, tap Accept, and their name immediately locks onto your family calendar.

Can I try Kid Pickup with our sports team calendars too? +

Yes. Paste your live ICS calendar link from TeamSnap, GameChanger, your school district, or Google Calendar and practices and games stay synced automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription if it isn’t right for us? +

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your Apple Account name, choose Subscriptions, and tap Cancel Subscription on Kid Pickup. It takes ten seconds with zero retention screens or hoops.
